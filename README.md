MindLock — A Mindful Focus & Phone Usage Tracker

MindLock is an Android application designed to help students and professionals stay mindful of their phone usage. Instead of restricting users, the app encourages positive focus habits by tracking unlock behavior, showing focus time, and rewarding consistency.

🚀 Features

Focus Time Tracking: Measures uninterrupted screen-free duration to help users understand their focus patterns.

Purpose-Based Unlock Logging: Users select why they unlocked their phone, improving self-awareness.

Reward System: Milestones unlock simple rewards or vouchers to keep the experience positive.

Minimal & Clean UI: Designed to feel supportive, not restrictive.

Fast & Lightweight: Built during a hackathon with performance and simplicity in mind.

🧠 How It Works

MindLock collects basic unlock behavior and focus durations. Users add a reason each time they unlock the device, helping them understand distraction patterns. The app then displays this data in a clear dashboard and rewards consistent focus.

No guilt. No restrictions. Just mindful usage.

🌟 Hackathon Context

MindLock was developed during the Hack Your Life event at Amrita Bengaluru, where the theme was to build a solution for a real-life problem within a limited time. The prototype was built in ~5 hours and presented to the jury.
